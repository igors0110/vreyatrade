Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

This policy describes how Vreya Trade OÜ (“we”, “us”) handles information when you use vreyatrade.com or contact us about wholesale supply. It is a general template and does not replace advice for your own jurisdiction.

Who we are

Vreya Trade OÜ, Narva mnt 7-636, Kesklinna linnaosa, Tallinn 10117, Estonia. Email: info@vreyatrade.com.

What we collect

This website does not use an account system, shopping cart, or analytics cookies. If you email us, we receive the address you write from, your message, and any files you attach, such as a parts list.

How we use it

We use that information to answer enquiries, prepare quotations, fulfil orders, and keep ordinary business records. We do not sell personal data.

How long we keep it

Enquiry emails are kept for as long as needed to handle the request and for ordinary accounting and legal retention periods.

Sharing

We may share details with carriers, suppliers, and professional advisers only where needed to deliver goods or meet a legal duty.

Your requests

To ask for a copy of the information we hold about you, or to ask us to correct or delete it, write to info@vreyatrade.com. We may need to keep some records where the law requires it.

Changes

We may update this page. The date above will change when we do.
