About us

Vreya Trade OÜ is a young and fast-growing company. We work with markets in Southeast Asia, North America and Europe.

Our goal is to become a leading wholesale supplier of spare parts for mobile devices.

  1. Southeast Asia — sourcing and supply.
  2. North America — wholesale buyers and partners.
  3. Europe — trade, documents and delivery.

How we work

We work with trade buyers, repair networks and distributors. Orders are confirmed in writing, with model, quantity and delivery terms agreed before shipment.

The company is registered in Estonia and operates from Tallinn.

  1. Send the list of parts, models and volumes to info@vreyatrade.com.
  2. We confirm availability, price and lead time.
  3. Shipment follows the agreed terms and documents.
